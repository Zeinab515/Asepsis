## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

### What is BIOPRESS Analytics?
**BIOPRESS Analytics** is a software-only analytics and clinical decision support system designed for hospital Infection Prevention and Control (IPC) committees. It transforms observational hand hygiene audit data into statistically honest, auditable, and interpretable indicators without requiring proprietary hardware or continuous surveillance sensors.

### The Problem It Solves
Hospitals routinely conduct direct observation audits of healthcare workers according to the World Health Organization (WHO) "5 Moments for Hand Hygiene." However, standard hospital analytics frequently commit several critical scientific and governance errors:
1. **Misleading Departmental Rankings**: Facilities frequently rank clinical wards or nursing shifts against one another on small percentage differences that are merely random sampling noise ($p > 0.05$).
2. **Target Leakage in Machine Learning**: Audit datasets often record what action was taken (e.g., hand washing vs. glove usage). Including these columns in predictive models causes mathematical target leakage, artificially inflating accuracy to ~100% while rendering the model clinically useless.
3. **Absence of Uncertainty Estimates**: Presenting raw percentages without sample size ($n$) and confidence intervals misleads clinicians into believing small cohorts have high precision.
4. **Punitive Misuse**: Audit statistics are sometimes inappropriately weaponized to discipline individual staff rather than addressing systemic workflow barriers.

**BIOPRESS Analytics** was engineered specifically to solve each of these problems through strict epidemiological rigor, transparent machine learning, role-based security, and uncompromising honesty in visualization.

## 3. Scientific Ground Rules & Critical Audit Findings

During the initial data audit phase, the engineering and epidemiological teams established fundamental ground rules that every layer of the codebase strictly enforces:

### 1. Target Leakage Columns (Strict Exclusion)
- **`HHaction`** (Hand Hygiene Action: Hand Wash `HW` or Hand Rub `HR`):
  - Populated in **all 202** compliant rows (`Complied = Yes`).
  - Empty in **all 715** non-compliant rows (`Complied = No`).
- **`Missedbutglove`** (Missed opportunity where gloves were worn instead):
  - Empty in **all 202** compliant rows (`Complied = Yes`).
  - Populated in **all 715** non-compliant rows (`Complied = No`).
- **Enforcement**: Both columns are deterministic mathematical functions of the target. They are **strictly barred** from all ML training views (`v_ml_features`) and cause runtime assertion errors if requested by ML pipelines.

### 2. Observation Sequence (`Opp`) Anomalies
- The `Opp` column runs roughly from $1$ to $917$, but ID **`788` appears twice** and ID **`787` is completely absent** (a human clerical typo in the source study).
- `Opp` is not a clinical feature. It is stored as an observation counter, and the database uses a composite key of `(row_number, import_id)` for guaranteed uniqueness.

### 3. Allowed Clinical Features
Only 4 features are permitted for modeling:
1. **`Ward`**: Medical, NICU, Ob/Gyn, Paediatric, Surgical (5 categories).
2. **`Profcat`**: Nurse, Medical doctor, Midwife, Other (4 categories).
3. **`Shift`**: Morning, After noon, Night (3 categories).
4. **`Indication`**: The WHO 5 Hand Hygiene Moments.

### 4. The Epidemiological Truth: Indication is the Sole Significant Driver
Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) tests of independence against compliance yield:
- **`Indication`**: $\chi^2 = 419.92, p < 0.001$ (**Statistically Significant**)
- **`Ward`**: $\chi^2 = 5.86, p = 0.21$ (**Not Significant**)
- **`Shift`**: $\chi^2 = 1.05, p = 0.59$ (**Not Significant**)
- **`Profcat`**: $\chi^2 = 0.63, p = 0.89$ (**Not Significant**)

```text
Compliance by Indication Moment:
1. After body fluid exposure:         75.8% (113/149)  CI: [68.4% – 82.0%]
2. After patient contact:             42.8% (71/166)   CI: [35.5% – 50.4%]
3. Before an aseptic procedure:        3.6% (7/195)    CI: [1.7% – 7.2%]
4. After patient surroundings:         3.3% (5/152)    CI: [1.4% – 7.5%]
5. Before patient contact:             2.4% (6/255)    CI: [1.1% – 5.0%]
```

Healthcare workers washed their hands frequently **after** touching fluids or patients (self-protection), but compliance dropped to $2–4\%$ **before** patient contact and aseptic procedures (patient protection). Differences across departments or shifts are mere sampling noise.

### 5. Ob/Gyn & Midwife Structural Confounding
In Debre Berhan hospital, **100% of Midwives were stationed in the Ob/Gyn ward**, and no midwives worked elsewhere. Consequently, `Ward = Ob/Gyn` and `Profcat = Midwife` are completely collinear and confounded.

### Track A: Database & Security

#### Database Schema & Relational Design
Implemented in [`backend/app/db/models.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/db/models.py):
- **`institutions`**: Represents the physical healthcare center (`Debre Berhan Referral Hospital`).
- **`wards`**: Five physical units with database `CHECK` constraints on valid names.
- **`data_imports`**: Stores raw file metadata, SHA-256 integrity hash, import timestamps, and JSON-encoded contract warning logs.
- **`hand_hygiene_opportunities`**: Core transactional table. Uses composite key `(import_id, row_number)` with `CHECK` constraints ensuring `complied IS NOT NULL` and categories match allowed enumerations.
- **`model_runs`**: Tracks reproducible ML experiments with git commit hash, hyperparameters, feature lists, and metric scores.
- **`users` & `audit_log`**: Secure user accounts and access logging.

#### Secured SQL Views
Implemented in [`backend/app/db/session.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/db/session.py):
- **`v_ml_features`**: Bridges the database directly to machine learning training. It selects only `ward`, `profcat`, `shift`, `indication`, and `complied`. By omitting `hh_action` and `missed_but_glove` at the SQL view boundary, accidental leakage is physically impossible.
- **`v_compliance_facts`**: Materializes aggregate counts for fast analytical queries.

#### Auditable CSV Import Pipeline
Implemented in [`backend/app/services/importer.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/services/importer.py):
1. **Pre-Flight Contract Verification**: Uploads are checked for missing columns, unexpected values, file size limits (5MB), and row limits (50,000).
2. **Automated Defect & Anomaly Discovery**:
   - `OPP_DUPLICATE`: Detects duplicated sequence 788.
   - `OPP_SEQUENCE_GAP`: Detects missing sequence 787.
   - `TARGET_LEAKAGE_HHACTION`: Validates target leakage.
   - `TARGET_LEAKAGE_MISSEDBUTGLOVE`: Validates non-compliance leakage.
   - `CONFOUNDING_WARD_PROFCAT`: Warns of midwife-ward collinearity.
3. **Atomic Transaction Commit**: Requires explicit preview approval token; executes all inserts inside a single database transaction.

#### Security & RBAC
Implemented in [`backend/app/core/security.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/core/security.py):
- **Role-Based Access Control**:
  - `admin`: Data import, CSV upload, ML re-training.
  - `analyst`: Analytics queries, ML inspection, report generation, CSV export.
  - `viewer`: Read-only overview and compliance visualizations.
- **Authentication**: Short-lived JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with HS256; password hashing with standard native `bcrypt`.
- **CSV Formula Injection (DDE) Prevention**: `sanitize_csv_cell()` automatically escapes cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@` by prepending `'`, preventing malicious command execution in Excel.
- **Privacy Thresholds**: Subgroups with $n < 5$ have their compliance rate withheld (`None`); subgroups with $n < 30$ are tagged with small-sample confidence interval warnings.

#### Scientific Wilson Score Interval
Implemented in [`backend/app/services/analytics.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/services/analytics.py):
$$\tilde{p} = \frac{p + \frac{z^2}{2n}}{1 + \frac{z^2}{n}} \pm \frac{z}{1 + \frac{z^2}{n}} \sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n} + \frac{z^2}{4n^2}}$$
The Wilson score interval provides reliable coverage even when $p$ is close to 0 or 1 and sample sizes are modest, avoiding the failure modes of the normal approximation.

### Track D: Machine Learning & Explainability (XAI)

Implemented in [`backend/app/ml/evaluate.py`](file:///C:/Users/zeina/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/biopress-analytics/backend/app/ml/evaluate.py):

#### Models Benchmarked
1. `DummyClassifier(strategy='prior')`: baseline prior probability model.
2. `LogisticRegression(C=1.0, max_iter=1000)`: primary transparent model.
3. `DecisionTreeClassifier(max_depth=3)`: readable shallow tree.
4. `RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, max_depth=5)`: non-linear comparison.

#### 3 Rigorous Validation Designs
1. **Repeated Stratified 5-Fold Cross Validation**: $5 \times 10 = 50$ folds to evaluate statistical stability.
2. **Order-Based Split by Opp (70% Train / 30% Test)**: Rough temporal proxy check, explicitly noting that Opp is interleaved across wards.
3. **Leave-One-Ward-Out CV**: Trains on 4 wards and tests on the 5th held-out ward to assess spatial generalizability across hospital environments.

#### Ablation Studies & Empirical Results
```text
Benchmark Comparison (Repeated 5-Fold CV):
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model               Ablation            ROC-AUC (Mean ± SD)   PR-AUC (Mean)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dummy (Baseline)    All Features        0.500 ± 0.000         0.220
LogisticRegression  Indication Only     0.886 ± 0.021         0.738
LogisticRegression  All 4 Features      0.907 ± 0.018         0.781
DecisionTree (d=3)  All 4 Features      0.882 ± 0.024         0.742
RandomForest        All 4 Features      0.923 ± 0.016         0.804
LogisticRegression  Without Indication  0.504 ± 0.038         0.224
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

#### Decision Rule & Model Recommendation
The difference between Random Forest ($0.923$) and Logistic Regression ($0.907$) is within the fold-to-fold error margin ($\pm 0.018$). Because complex non-linear ensemble models introduce opacity without clinical gain, **transparent Logistic Regression is recommended**.

#### Clinical Explainability (Odds Ratios)
Using Logistic Regression coefficients with clear reference categories:
- **Reference Categories**:
  - `Indication`: *"Before patient contact"*
  - `Ward`: *"Medical"*
  - `Shift`: *"Morning"*
  - `Profcat`: *"Nurse"*
- **Key Findings**:
  - `After body fluid exposure`: **$\text{OR} = 81.67$** ($95\%\text{ CI: }[39.35 – 169.51], p < 0.001$)
  - `After patient contact`: **$\text{OR} = 14.19$** ($95\%\text{ CI: }[7.29 – 27.63], p < 0.001$)
  - `Before aseptic procedure`: **$\text{OR} = 0.81$** ($95\%\text{ CI: }[0.31 – 2.09], p = 0.66$) — *Not statistically different from baseline.*

## 7. Ethical Governance, Privacy & Clinical Limitations

1. **Observational Hawthorne Effect**:
   Observational auditing involves human observers with clipboards or tablets. Healthcare personnel are aware of observation, artificially elevating compliance. True everyday baseline compliance is likely lower.
2. **Absence of Longitudinal Tracking**:
   The audit contains no timestamps, dates, worker IDs, or patient IDs. The 917 opportunities cannot be used to model time-series trends or individual habit formation.
3. **Absolute Prohibition on Disciplinary Action**:
   Compliance variations reflect systemic workflows, glove supply availability, patient contact urgency, and bedside sink access. Using model probabilities or subgroup compliance numbers to penalize individual staff is statistically invalid and ethically impermissible.
